What Are Pictos?

Pictos are the equipment system in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33. Each character can equip up to three Pictos at once, which provide both stat bonuses (Health, Defense, Speed, or Critical Rate) and a unique passive ability (the Lumina).

After using a Picto in four battles, all characters permanently learn its Lumina — the passive can then be equipped in a Lumina slot using Lumina Points (LP), freeing up the Picto slot for something else.

How To Get Pictos

  • Found in the world — scattered across every area, often tucked away in side paths
  • Bought from merchants — some require defeating the merchant in combat first
  • Dropped by enemies & bosses — certain bosses always drop specific Pictos
  • Quest rewards — completing side quests and challenges
  • DLC (Verso’s Drafts) — 17 new Pictos in the free Thank You Update

Tip: Duplicate Pictos you find will stack and upgrade the level of the one you already own, improving its stats.

Lumina Costs

Once a Lumina is learned, it can be equipped using Lumina Points (LP). Characters gain 1 LP per level-up; additional LP can be acquired using Colour of Lumina expedition resources.
